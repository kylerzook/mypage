About Me

I am a junior at the University of Missouri – Columbia pursuing a B.S. and an accelerated M.S. in Computer Science, with minors in Mathematics and Biological Sciences. My work sits where machine learning meets genomics, and what pulls me there is how directly these methods are changing the way we understand disease and design treatment.

In Dr. Jianlin Cheng's lab I develop methods for gene regulatory network inference, building the multi-source data pipelines, quality-control tooling, and evaluation infrastructure that graph neural network models are trained and measured on. I am co-first author on a NeurIPS 2026 submission currently under review, and first author on a benchmark manuscript in preparation.

Outside the lab I build systems: distributed job platforms, GPU kernels, and RAG pipelines. I am actively seeking a Summer 2027 internship in software engineering, AI/ML engineering, or computational biology.

Projects

01

GRN-Bench — Distributed Benchmark Platform

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • Redis
  • Celery
  • Docker
  • S3

Self-hosted service that runs multi-seed graph ML experiments as queued jobs, with idempotent writes verified against 500 duplicate submissions and forced worker kills. A normalized Postgres schema over 1.8M metric rows cut leaderboard p95 from 1.2 s to 35 ms, and content-addressed versioning deduplicated 47 GB to 12 GB. A full 300-run sweep drops from 14 h to 2.5 h across six workers.
