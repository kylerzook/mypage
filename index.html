01 About

Machine learning, meet genomics.

I am a junior at the University of Missouri — Columbia pursuing a B.S. and an accelerated M.S. in Computer Science, with minors in Mathematics and Biological Sciences. What pulls me toward this intersection is how directly these methods are changing the way we understand disease and design treatment.

In Dr. Jianlin Cheng's lab I develop methods for gene regulatory network inference — building the multi-source pipelines, quality-control tooling, and evaluation infrastructure that graph neural network models are trained and measured on. I am co-first author on a NeurIPS 2026 submission under review.

Outside the lab I build systems: distributed job platforms, GPU kernels, and retrieval pipelines. I care about the unglamorous layer — reproducibility, data integrity, and benchmarks you can actually trust.
