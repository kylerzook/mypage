02 About

About me.

I’m a junior at the University of Missouri–Columbia studying computer science through the accelerated B.S./M.S. program, with minors in mathematics and biological sciences. I’m interested in the point where computation can make biological questions easier to test.

In Dr. Jianlin Cheng’s lab, I work on gene regulatory network inference. My role includes building data pipelines, quality-control checks, and evaluation tools for graph neural network models. I am a co-first author on a paper currently under review at ICLR.

I also like systems work: job queues, GPU kernels, and retrieval pipelines. The less visible parts of a project matter to me—clean data, reproducible runs, and results that hold up when someone else tries them.

03 Research & Experience

Research and industry work.

Machine Learning Research — Gene Regulatory Network Inference

Aug 2025 – Present Dr. Jianlin Cheng's Lab · University of Missouri — Columbia
  • Built a pipeline that collects, normalizes, and version-controls genomic data from six public repositories. The resulting schema covers more than 200 cell types across human and mouse references.
  • Created automated QC checks that compare generated networks with reference standards before a model enters training.
  • Built the evaluation setup for GNN link prediction, including fixed splits, controlled negative sampling, and multi-seed AUROC/AUPRC reporting.
  • Developed the literature-validation agent and multi-omic validation component for a paper now under review at ICLR.
