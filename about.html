The Lore

I’m a junior studying computer science through Mizzou’s accelerated B.S./M.S. program. I’m interested in the point where computation makes biological questions easier to test.

In Dr. Jianlin Cheng’s lab I build machine learning methods for gene regulatory network inference — learning from genomic data which genes control which, and how that changes over time. I built the literature-verification module for EviGRN (NeurIPS 2026) and I’m co-first author on GRNPred, now in review.

I also like the unglamorous systems work: job queues, GPU kernels, retrieval pipelines. Clean data and runs someone else can reproduce matter more to me than a pretty number.
